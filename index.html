A small team building software for small sellers.

We are working on an online storefront for shops that sell on Instagram, so their business does not depend on one platform.

Current project

Sanjaab

Many small shops in Iran run their whole business from an Instagram page. If access to the page is lost, the shop is lost too.

Our platform lets a seller import their Instagram posts once. The posts are saved in our own database, which becomes the source of truth, so the storefront keeps working even if Instagram is disrupted.

Customers browse products, fill a cart, and place an order. To pay, they are sent to the seller's own payment method. Sellers manage their orders in one place.
